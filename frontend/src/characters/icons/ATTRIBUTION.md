# Bundled character icons

Small local copies, retrieved 2026-11-07, so the app makes no runtime third-party image requests.
GW2 and its icons are property of ArenaNet, LLC / NCSOFT; used here as game-data imagery.

- Profession glyphs (32x32): official GW2 render service (`render.guildwars2.com/file/...`), the
  `icon` URLs of the public `/v2/professions` API.
- Crafting discipline icons (20x20 "tango" icons): Guild Wars 2 Wiki (`wiki.guildwars2.com/images/...`,
  `File:<Discipline> tango icon 20px.png`), content under the wiki's licence terms.
